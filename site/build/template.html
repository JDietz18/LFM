Good to know

{{ORNAMENT}}
What is a Munchkin?
A small to medium cat with noticeably short legs from a naturally occurring gene, and an otherwise ordinary, playful, affectionate cat. They run, climb and get into everything; they just do it closer to the ground.
When can a kitten come home?
At 12 weeks, once they've had their first vaccinations and a vet check. Leaving mum and the litter a little later makes for a calmer, better-socialised kitten.
What comes with a kitten?
Vet records, a starter bag of the food they're used to, a blanket that smells like home, and our number for anything that comes up.
Do you ship or deliver?
We prefer families to collect in person so you can meet everyone. Ask us about delivery within a few hours' drive.
How do I follow along?
Facebook is where litters are announced first, Instagram has the daily photos, and TikTok has the little videos. Links are at the top and bottom of this page.
